# Xal's Routes Data - Changelog

## 1.0.0 - August 20, 2026

---

Had someone in Discord lose their whole node collection to a client crash - no fault of the addon, WoW just doesn't let anything force a save mid-session - and it got me thinking there should be a fallback. So this is that: a data pack of nodes I've already farmed out myself, ready to drop straight into your own Routes database in one click, no rebuilding from scratch required. Thanks for being part of the Xal family - hope it saves you some legwork.

### 🆕 New
- First data pack - 936 pre-collected gathering nodes across 22 zones (current expansion only for now, no Classic or older-expansion zones yet).
- Import it into Xal's Xpedited Routes with one click from the What's New splash, or anytime with `/xrd import`.

# Xal's Routes Data - Changelog

## 1.2.0 - October 25, 2026

---

Been trying to nail down a style I'm actually happy with for a while now - something minimalistic that still looks good, not just stripped down for the sake of it. I like darker coloring, so that's the direction I landed on: a deep brick red instead of the old orange, near-black indigo lines instead of the muted brown, and new fonts to match. I also kept farming the whole time, so the data pack's a good bit bigger too. Thanks for being part of the Xal family - hope you like where this is headed.

### 🆕 New
- Data pack refreshed - 1,361 pre-collected gathering nodes across 31 zones, up from 1,033 across 29.

### 🎨 Changed
- New look: dark brick-red accent, near-black indigo dividers and borders, new title/body fonts - matches the rest of the Xal's addons now.

## 1.1.0 - September 12, 2026

---

Been tinkering with how the whole suite looks lately, trying to give everything a cleaner, more cohesive feel - so this one's a style pass: swapped the old bronze-gold for a deep indigo-and-orange look that's becoming the new standard across all my addons. Caught a couple of bugs along the way too (hey, I'm only human) - one where the What's New splash's text was overlapping itself. Fixed both. Kept farming while I was at it, so there's a bigger data pack too. Thanks for being part of the Xal family - hope you like the new look.

### 🆕 New
- Data pack refreshed - 1,033 pre-collected gathering nodes across 29 zones, up from 936 across 22.

### 🎨 Changed
- New look: deep indigo background, burnt-orange accent - matches the rest of the Xal's addons now.

### 🔧 Fixed
- The What's New splash's text overlapping itself.
- The settings window and splash could land on top of another addon's window - now they remember where you last put them.

## 1.0.0 - August 20, 2026

---

Had someone in Discord lose their whole node collection to a client crash - no fault of the addon, WoW just doesn't let anything force a save mid-session - and it got me thinking there should be a fallback. So this is that: a data pack of nodes I've already farmed out myself, ready to drop straight into your own Routes database in one click, no rebuilding from scratch required. Thanks for being part of the Xal family - hope it saves you some legwork.

### 🆕 New
- First data pack - 770 pre-collected gathering nodes across 19 zones (current expansion only for now, no Classic or older-expansion zones yet).
- Import it into Xal's Xpedited Routes with one click from the What's New splash, or anytime with `/xrd import`.
